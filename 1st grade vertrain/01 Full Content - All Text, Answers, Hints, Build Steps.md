## Mission 2: Math Word Problems

1. Sam has 8 flags. He gets 6 more flags. How many flags does Sam have now?
   - *Skill:* Addition within 20
   - *Picture: 8 flags, a plus sign, and 6 more flags.*
   - **Answer:** 14 flags
   - **Clue if wrong:** Start at 8. Count up 6 more on your fingers.
2. Our class made 15 thank-you cards. We mailed 9 cards. How many cards are left?
   - *Skill:* Subtraction within 20
   - *Picture: 15 thank-you cards. 9 of them have an X on them.*
   - **Answer:** 6 cards
   - **Clue if wrong:** Start with all the cards. Take away the ones we mailed.
3. ★ Mia has 12 poppies. She gives 5 poppies to her grandpa. How many poppies does Mia have left?
   - *Skill:* Subtraction within 20
   - *Picture: 12 poppies. 5 of them have an X on them.*
   - **Answer:** 7 poppies
   - **Clue if wrong:** Draw 12 circles. Cross out 5. Count what is left.
4. Ben has 13 stars. Ava has 9 stars. How many more stars does Ben have than Ava?
   - *Skill:* Compare: how many more
   - *Picture: a row of 13 stars for Ben. Under it, a row of 9 stars for Ava.*
   - **Answer:** 4 more stars
   - **Clue if wrong:** Line up the two rows. Count the stars that have no partner.
5. Each box holds 10 flags. There are 4 boxes. How many flags are there in all?
   - *Skill:* Count by tens
   - *Picture: 4 boxes. Each box says 10.*
   - **Answer:** 40 flags
   - **Clue if wrong:** Count by tens. Say one number for each box.
6. The parade has 5 red flags, 4 white flags, and 6 blue flags. How many flags are there in all?
   - *Skill:* Add three numbers
   - *Picture: 5 red flags, 4 white flags, and 6 blue flags.*
   - **Answer:** 15 flags
   - **Clue if wrong:** Add two colors first. Then add the last color.

**Lock number 2 = 7.** It is the answer to the ★ problem (problem 3).

## Google Form Build Guide (step by step)

You need a free Google account. There are two ways to build the form. Both make the same form.

### Way 1: Automatic (about 2 minutes)

1. Go to script.google.com and sign in. Click **New project**.
2. Delete the lines already in the editor.
3. Open **Google Form Auto-Builder.txt**, copy all of it, and paste it into the editor.
4. Click **Save** (floppy disk icon), then **Run**.
5. Allow permissions: **Review permissions** > your account > **Advanced** > **Go to project** > **Allow**. The warning appears because you wrote the script yourself.
6. When it says "Execution completed", open the log. Copy the **EDIT link** (for you) and the **STUDENT link** (for your class).

### Way 2: By hand (about 15 minutes)

1. Go to forms.google.com and click **Blank form**. Paste the form title and description from the table below.
2. Click the first question. Change its type to **Short answer**. Paste the name question and turn **Required** on.
3. For each mission, click **Add section** in the side toolbar (two stacked bars). Paste the section title and description.
4. Add each question with the **+** button. Choose **Short answer**, paste the question, and turn **Required** on.
5. Click the **three dots** at the bottom right of the question and choose **Description**. Paste the help line.
6. Click the three dots again and choose **Response validation**.
   - Letter and word answers: choose **Regular expression**, then **Matches**, and paste the pattern.
   - Math answers: choose **Number**, then **Equal to**, and type the number.
   - Paste the clue into **Custom error text**. Students see it in red when an answer is wrong.
7. After the code question, add the last section, "You did it!".
8. Click **Settings** > **Presentation**. Turn on **Show progress bar**. Paste the celebration line as the **Confirmation message**.
9. Click the **eye** icon to preview. Type a wrong answer to check the clue. Type the right one to check that **Next** works.
10. Optional: add pictures from the **Google Form Images** folder with the image icon next to a question.
11. Share: click **Send** > **link** icon > **Copy**, and post the link in Google Classroom. Keep the edit link private.

**Why students cannot skip ahead:** every answer is Required and has response validation. Google Forms will not let a student click **Next** until every answer in the section is right. The next section then shows the lock number.
