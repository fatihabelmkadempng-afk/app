# Clip Art Descriptions: Mission: Thank a Hero!

Every picture in the PDFs is original art, drawn with simple shapes in `source/art.js`. You can sell the product as it is. If you want to make polished versions in an image generator, use the descriptions below. Replace the art in the PDFs only with images you made or have a commercial license for.

**Style for every image:** flat vector clip art, thick dark navy outline (#1B2330), no gradients, no text, white background, kid-friendly and rounded. Colors are navy #1E3A6E, red #C62F3B, gold #E3A72F, and cream #FFF6E6. Each image must still read when printed in black and white.
